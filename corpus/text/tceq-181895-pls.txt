The following summary is provided for this pending air permit application being reviewed by the Texas
Commission on Environmental Quality as required by 30 Texas Administrative Code Chapter 39. The information
provided in this summary may change during the technical review of the application and are not federal
enforceable representations of the permit application.

Energy Forge One LLC (EFO) has submitted an air construction permit application to request authorization for a
greenfield natural gas-fired turbine Power Plant. The proposed Power Plant (Kilby site) is located near Pecos,
Reeves County, Texas. EFO is submitting a Core Data Form as part of this application to be assigned a Texas
Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) Customer Reference Number (CN606441046) and Regulated
Entity Number (RN112304498).
