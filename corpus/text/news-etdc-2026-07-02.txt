* Investment supports Project Kilby, a 2.67 GW generation campus in Texas.
Joulent's first project, Project Kilby, will be a 2.67 gigawatt (GW) natural gas-powered generation campus in Reeves County, Texas, developed in collaboration with Chevron subsidiary Energy Forge and GE Vernova. This facility is set to supply power to a Microsoft-operated data center campus under a 20-year power purchase agreement (PPA). Joulent has also secured a 50% ownership stake in Project Kilby.
